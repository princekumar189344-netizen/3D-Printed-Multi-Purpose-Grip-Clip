# Grip Clip - 3D Modelling Journal

## Project Name

**3D Printed Grip Clip**

## Project Type

3D Modelling / CAD

## Project Duration

**About 7 Hours**

---

# Part 1 - Basic Planning

## Time: 1 Hour

### Objective

The first part was to understand the grip clip and plan how I would make it in CAD software.

### Work Done

I used a real blue grip clip as the reference for my model.

I looked at the shape from different sides and tried to understand how the two arms, middle section and hinge work.

I also made a small rough sketch before starting the CAD model.

I decided to make the model in small steps instead of making the whole thing at one time.

### Problems

The shape was a little difficult to understand because the clip has two arms and a hinge in the middle.

### What I Learned

I learned that looking at the real object from different views helps when making a 3D model.

### Result

The basic idea and rough shape were ready.

---

# Part 2 - Basic 3D Shape

## Time: 1.5 Hours

### Objective

The objective was to make the main body of the grip clip.

### Work Done

I opened the CAD software and created a new file.

First I made the basic shape of the clip. I made the two main sides and the middle opening.

I also added a simple hole in the body.

I did not add the hinge or grip details at this stage because I wanted to keep the first model simple.

I checked the model from the front, top and side views.

### Problems

At first some of the sizes were not correct, so I changed the dimensions.

### What I Learned

I learned how to make basic sketches and extrude them into a 3D shape.

### File

(grip_clip_step1_basic_shape_clean.stl)

(grip_clip_step1_basic_design.step)

### Result

The basic body of the grip clip was completed.

---

# Part 3 - Grip and Hinge Area

## Time: 2 Hours

### Objective

The objective was to add the grip section and make the hinge area.

### Work Done

After completing the basic body, I started working on the grip section.

I made the upper parts of the two arms and changed their shape to look more like the real clip.

After this I worked on the hinge area.

I made hinge sections on both sides of the clip. The two parts were aligned so they could work with one hinge.

I also made long thin holes in the hinge sections.

I checked the holes from different directions to make sure the hinge pin could pass through.

I used the hinge pin to check whether the two parts fit together and could move.

### Problems

The hinge area was difficult because the two parts needed to be lined up.

I had to change the position of the holes a few times.

### What I Learned

I learned how important alignment is when making parts that need to move together.

### File

(grip_clip_step2_grip_section.stl)

(grip_clip_step2_grip_section.step)

(grip_clip_step3_spring_hinge.stl)

(grip_clip_step3_hinge.step)

### Result

The grip section and basic hinge area were completed.

---

# Part 4 - Size and Design Changes

## Time: 1.5 Hours

### Objective

The objective was to check the model and make some changes to the sizes and design.

### Work Done

I compared the CAD model with the real grip clip again.

I noticed that some parts were too thick and some parts needed more space.

I changed the size of the arms and adjusted the middle section.

I also changed the hinge area slightly so that the two sides were better aligned.

I checked the model again from different angles.

After making the changes, the model looked closer to the real grip clip.

### Problems

Some changes affected other parts of the model, so I had to check the model again after changing the dimensions.

### What I Learned

I learned that changing one dimension can sometimes affect other parts of a model.

### File

(grip_clip_step4_design_fix.stl)

(grip_clip_step4_size_and_desgin_changes.step)

### Result

The model was improved and the main size problems were fixed.

I opened the latest version of the model and checked all the main parts.

# Problems I Faced

During the project I faced some small problems.

1. Getting the basic shape correct.
2. Making the two arms the correct size.
3. Making the hinge parts line up.
4. Making the hinge holes correctly.
5. Changing the dimensions without disturbing other parts.
6. Making the final model look closer to the real grip clip.

I fixed these problems by changing the model and checking it again.
